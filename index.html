After the PvP

Win, then exit clean

The vote is only the entry. Once the other coin is dead, Anchor does not dump the winner into the chart.

Winner gets the buy

50% of the active treasury buys the voted coin. That is the anti-PvP punch.

Wait for the other side to die

When the losing ticker is effectively dead, the protocol starts taking profit — not at the wick.

Responsible DCA out

Sells are split across time so the chart is not smashed. Realized SOL goes back to the chest for the next PvP.
